# Digi Night: signature moves (build 17z33)

Every line has moves only it learns, from Baby to Mega. Signature moves all fire on their own timers.

- **Grows**: learning it replaces the move it grows from (and keeps about three quarters of its levels). It needs that move at Lv 3.
- **Choice**: at Rookie you take one of two. The other, and everything it would grow into (Champion, Ultimate, Mega), is locked for the rest of the run.
- **Keeper**: stays as it is for good.
